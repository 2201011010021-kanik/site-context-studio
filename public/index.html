ABOUT ME

Curiosity, drawn
to scale.

I’m interested in how a place can be read before it is redesigned. Kaniks Studio brings site data, drawings and spatial thinking into one working space.

Architecture begins with observation. The map is a starting point: buildings, streets, terrain, trees and the gaps in what we know.

This studio is an evolving design tool. It lets you choose a boundary, check the evidence behind a layer and make graphics you can take into your own work.
